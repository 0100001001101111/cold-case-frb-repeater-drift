# COLD CASE AST-04 — Do repeating fast radio bursts drift? CHIME's public data cannot say

Family: astronomy  |  Disposition: **POSTABLE_AS_COLD_CASE**  |  claim_authority=NONE
Members: chime-repeater-dm-boundary-v1, chime-second-representation-v1, chime_selection_followup_v1, chime-source-specific-dm-raw-refit-v1

## The case
If repeating FRB dispersion measures drift - together, or per source - that is either plasma physics around the sources or a clock on their environments. Four frozen routes attacked it from the public CHIME catalogs.

## How it died
The shared-boundary test collapsed from 22 sources to ONE testable boundary once calendar overlap was demanded (catch C360: burst counts hide overlap collapse); the strongest development pattern was matched by 433 of 5,001 chance worlds against a 50-world pass line; raw-spectrum refits produced zero usable candidates (32 of 39 early spectra failed quality gates); and the released injection table cannot say whether a physical source even enters the catalog or earns the repeater label - the selection function is unpublished.

## What still stands
Bounded nulls at every tested scale plus a precise statement of the missing product: no occupied-bandwidth variable, no source-level selection model, insufficient calendar overlap. Ceiling: released catalogs.

## What would revive it
A release-bound source-observation product: per-source observing windows, burst-arrival behavior, label-assignment rules, bandwidth response - or a longer catalog with ten-plus overlapping repeaters around predeclared instrument epochs.

## Card fields (G18 contract)
- plain_meaning: Whether repeating radio bursts drift is unanswerable from what CHIME has released, not because the data is bad but because the exposure bookkeeping that gives counts their meaning is internal.
- what_it_is_not: Not a criticism of CHIME, because selection products are hard and internal by default; and not evidence of no drift, because the tests were exposure-starved, not negative.
- next_decisive_test: The frozen boundary test on the first release with a source-level selection function.
- what_we_need: CHIME/FRB collaboration: per-source observing-window and selection products; even a rough exposure table per repeater would reopen two routes.
